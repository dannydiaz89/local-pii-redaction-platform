# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, safe, and equitable community. Everyone participating in this
project is expected to treat other people with dignity and to value their contributions regardless
of identity, background, experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from the experience.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the safety-sensitive nature of privacy tooling.
- Use synthetic data in project discussions, examples, issues, and tests.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, unwanted sexual attention, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without
permission are not acceptable. Participants must not use project spaces to expose real personal
information, credentials, private documents, or vulnerability details.

## Reporting a concern

The project maintainer and current community moderator is
[dannydiaz89](https://github.com/dannydiaz89).

Until the public repository provides a dedicated private conduct-reporting channel, use that
profile to request private contact instructions. Do not include the incident, personal data, or
other sensitive details in a public issue, discussion, or profile message. Security vulnerabilities
must be reported according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. The maintainer will consider the
safety and privacy of everyone involved, investigate in good faith, disclose information only when
needed to address the concern, and communicate the outcome when appropriate. A maintainer who is
personally involved in a report must seek a neutral reviewer before taking enforcement action.

## Enforcement

Responses will be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, and any pattern of prior conduct. They
may include a private warning, limits on project activities, temporary suspension, or permanent
removal from project spaces. Where it is safe and appropriate, the response may also include steps
to acknowledge and repair harm. Retaliation against a person who makes or assists with a good-faith
report is prohibited.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and whenever a person is officially representing the
project. It also applies to conduct outside project spaces when that conduct creates a credible
safety risk for project participants.
